### 🛸 I. RHYTHMIC CYBERNETICS: BEYOND THE GRID

Forget static 16-step loops. In **Extasis Rhythm**, time is elastic, polymetric, and stochastic:

* **12 Autonomous Polymetric Channels:** Every single voice runs on its own independent clock division, sequence length (1 to 32 steps), and metric topology. Run a 5-step sub-bass against a 7-step kick and a 12-step syncopated snare for perpetual, non-repeating rhythmic interference patterns.
* **4 Sequencing Topologies:** Execute sequences **Forward**, **Inverted (Reverse)**, **Stochastic (Random)**, or in self-intersecting **Ping-Pong (Mirror)** cycles per channel.
* **Euclidean "FIX" & Triplet Acceleration:** Instantly condense or stretch patterns across musical bars without losing downbeat synchronization.
* **Micro-Division Ratcheting:** Sub-divide any step into 2x or 3x bursts for drill rolls, glitch flurries, and footwork syncopations.

---

### 🧬 II. THE 12-CHANNEL ARCHITECTURE

Each voice is an independent hybrid channel strip designed for maximum punch, transient authority, and harmonic saturation:

* **Smart Transient Shaper:** Dedicated per-channel Attack and Decay envelope modeling with anti-click zero-crossing DSP.
* **Dynamic TPT Dual-Mode Filtering:** State-variable lowpass and highpass filtering with resonant sweep.
* **Analog Drive & Velocity Accent:** Individual saturation circuits that react dynamically to step velocity.
* **Micro-Tuning & Step Transposition:** Real-time chromatic pitch shifting (-24 to +24 semitones) per step or per channel.
* **Direct Aux Routing:** Independent send busses to the internal Reverb and Delay networks.
* **Dedicated Mute & Solo Matrix:** Instant console-grade track muting and amber-gold channel isolation with clickless gain interpolation.

---

### 🎛️ III. THE STOCHASTIC FILL MATRIX (THE GLITCH CIRCUIT)

Inject spontaneous chaotic mutations without breaking the dancefloor:
* Independent 16-step generative fill sequencer.
* Automated burst injection with directional shift controls (Shift Left / Right).
* Triplet warp and length compression for accelerating rolls and machine-gun breakdowns.

---

### 📻 IV. THE DEGRADATION RACK & MASTER PROCESSOR

Route your rhythm through a chain of vintage degradation stages and studio bus hardware:

* **PCM Bitcrusher & Rate Decimator:** Continuously morph from pristine 24-bit audio down to crunchy 12-bit SP-1200 bite and brutal 8-bit industrial quantization.
* **Resonant Spring Tank Reverb:** Non-linear triple-spring acoustic simulation with drive saturation and decay modulation.
* **BPM-Synced Tape Delay:** Subdivided echoes with analog flutter, high-feedback resonance, and stereo widening.
* **Zero-Crossing Flanger & Space Chorus:** Deep analog chorus and jet-engine comb modulation for hats and percussion.
* **Dynamic Sidechain Pump:** Internal kick-triggered sidechain compression for that heavy French touch / pumping club breathing effect.
* **Analog Tape & Vinyl Master:** Introduces vinyl micro-dust, harmonic warmth, and analog tape compression.
* **Hardware Ceiling Limiter & Soft Clipper:** Transparent peak control and aggressive wave-folding saturation that pushes loops to maximum loudness without harsh digital overs.

### ⚡ V. WORKFLOW VELOCITY: DRAG WAV TO DAW

* **Vector Waveform Drag-and-Drop (`DRAG WAV`):** Grab the animated waveform button and drop a perfectly trimmed, 24-bit sample-accurate audio loop directly onto your DAW's arrangement timeline (Ableton Live, FL Studio, Logic Pro, Reaper, Bitwig, Cubase) or your desktop.
* **Smart Sample Classifier:** Drag your own custom WAV, AIFF, or MP3 files onto any channel. Extasis Rhythm analyzes the frequency spectrum and Levenshtein tag metadata to auto-categorize and balance the sound automatically.
* **Kit Collect & Bundle:** Hit `COLLECT KIT` to package all custom samples and parameters into an autonomous portable folder ready for live sets or archive sharing.
* **Pattern Morphing:** Store and switch between 8 complete pattern banks (A through H) on the fly for live performance and complex arrangements.
